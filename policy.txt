Policy Title: Apex Consumer Lending Policy
Product: Unsecured Personal Loan Guidelines
Classification: Internal Underwriting Standard (Synthetic / Non-Commercial)

Eligibility & Underwriting Criteria:
1. Minimum Income Requirement:
   - Verified net monthly income must be at least ₹35,000 ($2,000 per month).
2. Credit Score Threshold:
   - Minimum qualifying credit bureau score is 680.
3. Debt Burden & EMI Obligations:
   - Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (existing monthly EMIs plus proposed new EMI) must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.
4. Loan Amount Eligibility:
   - Requested loan amount must not exceed 10 times the applicant's verified monthly net income.
   - Applications for loan amounts above ₹10,00,000 ($12,000) require a minimum credit score of 740.

Decision Rules:
- Approve: Applicant satisfies minimum income (>= ₹35,000), minimum credit score (>= 680), total EMIs <= 50% of income, and requested loan amount <= 10x monthly income.
- Reject: Applicant fails any of the above thresholds or has excessive existing debt obligations.
